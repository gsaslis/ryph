Choosing a queue for job dispatch

Three options compared on latency, operations and cost for about 2,000 jobs a minute.

8 October 2026 Requested in #platform

TL;DR

Use Postgres with SKIP LOCKED. It meets the latency target with the database we already run, and adds no new service to operate. Revisit at ten times the load.

  • 2,000jobs per minute at peak
  • 38 msp95 dispatch, Postgres
  • 0new services to run

What we found

All three options meet the 100 ms dispatch target at today's load. They differ in what they cost to run and in how they fail.1

Postgres keeps jobs and their state in one transaction, so a crash between "claimed" and "done" cannot lose a job. Redis Streams is faster but needs its own persistence setup to match that.2
